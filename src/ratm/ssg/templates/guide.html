How to read this model

Each threat describes something that could go wrong, the components it applies to, how likely and how damaging it is, and what is being done about it.

Threat status

The status records how the team is handling a threat. Open threats are the ones that still need work. They are counted and ranked separately on the summary.

After mitigations

Each threat can also record the impact and likelihood that remain once its mitigations are in place. The risk computed from those is the risk after mitigations, also called residual risk. When a threat records nothing different, it equals the original risk. Lists on this site put the highest risk after mitigations first. The residual risk note on a threat page explains what is left.

Where threats apply

Each component has properties, such as whether it is exposed to the network. A threat applies to every component whose properties meet all of its requirements. Scenarios show those components in context. The model internals pages show the rules themselves.

Tags

A label such as Legacy next to a name is a tag set by the model's authors, for example to mark parts of an older setup. Components with a tag are drawn with a dashed outline in the diagrams. A threat carries a tag when every component it applies to has that tag, and a mitigation carries a tag when every threat that lists it does. Tags do not change which threats apply or how risk is counted.
